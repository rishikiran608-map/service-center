Our Story

From a Small Workshop to Anantapur's #1 Service Center

IFB Service Center Anantapur was started in 2010 by Mr. V. Ramesh, a factory-trained technician with a simple belief: honest service at a fair price. What began as a one-man workshop on Main Road has grown into a team of 8 certified technicians serving thousands of families.

Over 15 years we have repaired more than 10,000 appliances — always using genuine OEM parts and always standing behind our work with a 90-day warranty.

Our customers keep coming back and referring friends because we treat every appliance as if it belongs to someone we know personally.
