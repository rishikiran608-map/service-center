Our Story

From a Small Workshop to Anantapur's #1 Service Center

IFB Service Center Anantapur was started in 2010 by Mr. V. Ramesh, a factory-trained appliance technician with a simple belief: honest service at a fair price. What began as a one-man workshop on Main Road has grown into a team of 8 certified technicians serving thousands of families across Anantapur district.

Over 15 years we have repaired and installed more than 10,000 washing machines — Front Load, Top Load, and Semi-Automatic — always using genuine OEM spare parts and standing behind our work with a 90-day warranty.

Our customers keep coming back — and they refer their friends and family — because we treat every appliance as if it belongs to someone we know personally. That philosophy hasn't changed since day one.
